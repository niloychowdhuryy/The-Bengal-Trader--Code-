# Game Art Direction

Keep the game visually coherent: premium illustrated historical Bengal, with a consistent material world, level of detail, palette, and finish across screens and assets.

## Workflow

1. Inspect existing approved art, asset references, UI styling, and game setting before creating or changing visuals. Extend established decisions rather than introducing an unrelated style.
2. Define each asset's purpose, period and regional context, point of view, silhouette, scale, palette, lighting, materials, and level of detail before implementation.
3. Apply shared visual rules: maps should communicate geography and routes clearly; boats, characters, and buildings should have distinctive, historically informed forms; environments should support the same Bengal setting.
4. Use a deliberate palette, consistent light direction, layered texture, and controlled detail. Maintain contrast and readability at the asset's actual mobile display size.
5. For animation, specify timing, easing, weight, and a clear gameplay purpose. Keep motion consistent with the illustrated rendering and avoid distracting loops.
6. Compare new work against representative existing assets at screen size. Revise any mismatch in rendering, palette, lighting, texture, proportions, or historical context.

## Requirements and restrictions

- Maintain a polished, premium illustrated historical style across maps, boats, characters, buildings, environments, and effects.
- Keep shapes and details purposeful, readable, and appropriate to the game's historical and regional context.
- Preserve visual hierarchy and legibility in portrait mobile layouts; art must support gameplay rather than obscure controls or information.
- Avoid primitive geometric placeholder artwork, generic stock-looking assets, inconsistent rendering, and unrelated visual styles.
- Do not use decorative detail as a substitute for clear silhouettes, composition, or a cohesive art direction.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Does the asset match the established illustrated Bengal art direction?
- [ ] Are palette, lighting, textures, detail, and rendering consistent with related assets?
- [ ] Are forms and details historically and regionally appropriate?
- [ ] Is it readable at mobile display size and does it support gameplay hierarchy?
- [ ] Are animations purposeful and visually consistent?
