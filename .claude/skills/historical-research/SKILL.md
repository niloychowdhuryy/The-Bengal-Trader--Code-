# Historical Research

Verify historical context before adding or changing historical content. Present documented history accurately and distinguish it clearly from fictionalised storytelling.

## Workflow

1. Define the claim or event and its relevant time period and location. Check the game's existing timeline and setting for conflicts.
2. Verify dates, place names, rulers, battles, kingdoms, political developments, trade routes, and context against reliable historical sources. Prefer scholarly books, academic publications, museums, archives, and reputable primary-source collections.
3. Cross-check important or disputed details with more than one credible source. Note uncertainty, variant spellings, approximate dates, and disagreements instead of presenting them as settled fact.
4. Separate documented facts from interpretation and fictionalised events inspired by history. Label fictional characters, dialogue, outcomes, and scenarios so players are not misled.
5. Consider regional context and multiple perspectives. Describe political or military conflict specifically; never portray an entire ethnic or religious community as inherently hostile, violent, or inferior.
6. Record concise source details alongside research notes or content where the project supports citations. If a claim cannot be verified, qualify it or omit it.

## Requirements and restrictions

- Check historical accuracy before implementation; do not rely on memory when dates, identities, or context matter.
- Do not collapse distinct kingdoms, communities, periods, or political actors into one.
- Avoid anachronistic labels and assumptions; use period-appropriate framing where possible and explain modern terms when needed.
- Treat uncertainty and fictionalisation transparently. Do not invent citations or imply that a dramatic game event is documented history.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Are dates, names, places, rulers, and political context checked against credible sources?
- [ ] Are disputed or approximate details appropriately qualified?
- [ ] Can players distinguish documented history from fictionalised events?
- [ ] Does the portrayal avoid stereotyping whole ethnic or religious communities?
- [ ] Are sources recorded and claims proportionate to the available evidence?
